Taco Tuesday line rehearsal

Does more RAM always mean a faster computer? Pick your part and run the show from your phone.

  • Tap Next line to move through the script, or tap any line to jump to it.
  • The cue light turns amber, then red, then lights up in your colour when it is your turn.
  • Auto mode reads other people’s lines at a set pace and waits for you on yours.
  • Switch on Hide my lines to test whether you know them.
